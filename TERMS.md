## 1. General-audience software

OWT is a general-audience productivity and language-learning tool. It is not specifically directed to children under 13 and does not operate an OWT account or hosted user-content service.

## 2. Third-party services

OWT can connect to third-party translation providers selected by the user, including Google Translate, Gemini, DeepL, Chrome Built-in AI, Ollama, Local HTTP, and user-configured Custom HTTP endpoints.

Those services are operated by their respective providers and may be subject to separate terms, privacy policies, quotas, fees, availability limits, and regional restrictions.

OWT is not affiliated with or endorsed by Netflix, YouTube, Google, DeepL, Chrome, Ollama, or other third-party services referenced for interoperability.

## 3. User-provided credentials and endpoints

Users are responsible for API keys, provider accounts, self-hosted services, and Custom HTTP endpoints they configure. Do not enter credentials into untrusted endpoints.

OWT attempts to keep raw credentials inside the extension sandbox and restricts Local HTTP/Ollama providers to loopback as documented, but users remain responsible for the services they choose to run or contact.

## 4. Translation accuracy

Machine translation and AI-generated explanations can be inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading. OWT is not intended to provide professional legal, medical, financial, safety-critical, or other expert advice.

Users should verify important translations with qualified sources before relying on them.

## 5. Website and player compatibility

Websites and streaming services can change their DOM, player interfaces, subtitle formats, or network behavior without notice. YouTube/Netflix integration may temporarily stop working after a platform update.

OWT does not bypass subscription requirements, DRM, or access controls.

## 6. No hosted user-content platform

Current OWT releases store vocabulary, settings, cache, and learning state locally in the user's browser profile. OWT does not currently provide a public upload/community hosting service.
